Beat Box

Say the beat out loud. Then sing your song over it.

A Drums take turns mouth sounds into real drums: low for the kick, sharp for the snare, a hiss for the hi-hat. A Voice take plays exactly as you sang it, on top of the beat. The metronome counts you in for one bar. Wear headphones so the click and the beat stay out of the take.

The beat

Loop

Every track plays together. Drums loop; voice takes play once, from the bar they come in at. Tap squares to add or remove hits. Faded squares repeat a shorter take. Space bar plays. Set the tempo before you sing: voice takes don't stretch.

Take it to Logic

Save everything gives you one file per track (voices as you sang them, the drums as audio and MIDI) plus the mix, all starting at bar 1. In Logic, set the tempo to match, then drag each file to bar 1 of its own track.

The MIDI file has one drum track per Beat Box track. Drag it into Logic and pick any Drum Kit.
